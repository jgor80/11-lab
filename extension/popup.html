PRICE CHECK

0of 30 searches used today
Last hour 0 · 3 hours 0 · 24 hours 0
Run one search yourself first, then press Start.

With the Web App open and signed in, the helper searches your club players that have no price from the last 12 hours, one every 10–20 seconds, with a few minutes' break every 12 searches. Caps: 20 an hour, and 30 a day on the first day, rising by 10 a day to 100. With Run automatically on, it starts by itself (after your first search in the tab) and checks again about every 10 minutes within those caps; Stop also turns that off. It only searches: it never bids, buys or lists. It stops by itself at any captcha, "too many actions" or error, and after EA limits or checks the account it pauses for 24 hours. Keep the Web App tab open while it runs.

Automatic searching carries more account risk than capturing your own searches. EA can restrict market access for it.

Any third-party software on EA's site carries some account risk.
